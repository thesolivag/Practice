# Preregistered research protocol v1 — 2026-11-14
Market: SPY, unlevered long-only regular-session trades, $100,000 starting capital, fractional sizing for research. No previous user trading methods used.
Budget: 3 hypotheses x 2 variants; one development/validation batch, at most one conditional final batch; 2 hours elapsed work and <=60 Actions runner minutes. Targeted checks local only. No expansion or tuning after failure.
Data: Yahoo chart query2 endpoint daily OHLC, dividend/split events, 2010-01-01 through 2025-12-31. Snapshot SHA256 e4e49bb5d439b63dd201b6f2285221fa9abf2ff3783920edf89e9a8ee1f9f10e. Retrospectively vendor-adjusted data is a limitation: same-day OHLC ratios and consecutive split-free closes only, no adjusted-close total-return signals. Require independent vendor cross-check before paper testing. Final data may be archived but no final performance or statistics viewed before validation and audit pass.
Splits: development 2010–2017; validation 2018–2021; final 2022–2025. Trailing prior history may warm signals. No performance-based date selection.
Rules (all evaluated from completed prior close): prior close/previous close -1 <= -0.005 or -0.01 (short-term reversal); >=0.005 or 0.01 (continuation); prior close > prior trailing SMA100 or SMA200 (trend persistence). Exactly six variants, all registered including failures. No shorting.
At each session open, allocate all available equity only if prior signal true; exit same session official close using MOC instruction committed before cutoff. Open/close prints are proxies, not guaranteed executable prices. Adverse spread 0.5bp + commission 0.5bp + slippage 1bp per side = 4bp round trip; stress doubles to 8bp. No volume participation model: $100k is research scale; opening/closing auction eligibility and broker MOC cutoff must be confirmed before paper testing. No stops or targets; overnight flat so no financing, dividend entitlement or overnight gap P&L; opening gaps change entry price. No stop/target sequencing ambiguity. Halts, rejected/unavailable auction orders unresolved in daily data; no assumed stop protection. No cash interest, taxes or leverage. Daily OHLC cannot verify auction availability, quotes or actual fills; results are screening evidence only.
Success gates on validation and conditional final: >=100 trades; annualized net Sharpe >=0.7 (252, daily zeros included); max drawdown <=20%; >=3 positive calendar years of 4; positive net sum excluding five best trade days; stress net cumulative return >0; 95% two-sided percentile CI lower bound for mean monthly return >0 using 5000 circular 3-month block bootstrap samples, deterministic seed 20261114. Neighbor variant must pass every gate. Select one development-best Sharpe variant across families before examining validation. No replacement after selected candidate fails. Family stability reported for all variants. Gross/net, monthly, trades, expectancy, drawdown, streaks, exposure and concentration reported for both observed partitions. No claim of statistical discovery after six comparisons. Pass warrants further data validation and frozen-rule forward paper proposal, not live deployment.
Stop: data-quality failure; selected candidate or neighbor fails validation; independent audit unresolved; unavailable realistic execution assumptions; or budget exhausted. If validation fails, do not compute final metrics. No final retuning.

Audit clarifications (before results): drawdown gate is end-of-day equity drawdown; intraday drawdown remains unmeasured, exposure is session fraction plus approximate wall-clock exposure (half-days not weighted). Profit concentration is summed trade percentage returns, not dollar P&L. Fractional auction execution unverified; positive findings cannot advance until whole-share order feasibility and independent source verification are resolved. XNYS session dates checked using exchange-calendars 4.13.3. Bootstrap CI is pointwise and not multiplicity adjusted.
