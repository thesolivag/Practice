Closed reaction and confirmation boundary

The entry contract (Causal clocks §2–3) allows patterns observed during a reaction candle and requires model-specific closing evidence. Full entry study E14 426.7–533.7 describes H4 KL/IRL context followed by a lower engulf and the new A/V. Neither establishes a universal requirement to wait an extra lower candle after a completed rejection.

Implementation choice: the identical closed lower bar may supply both reaction evidence and completing confirmation. Other reactions must end by candidate start, and all prerequisites must be available. The containing H4 is never declared closed by this change. Earlier known parent/KL, known liquidity, first-take linkage, terminal role loss, source profile, strict engulf geometry and risk/target checks remain enforced.

Regression covers a witnessed earlier pool take followed by same-bar reaction/break confirmation, the later-bar alternative, and a prefix without the completing candle. Risk is stubbed only to isolate entry timing; historical replay verifies real protection separately. This fixes an unnecessary time boundary; it does not establish source chart identity or exact teacher acceptance.
