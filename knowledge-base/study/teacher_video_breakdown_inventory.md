## What a primary replay must establish

The initial acceptance tasks are ordered: independently identify the named source candles and feed/anchor, establish bias and H4 breakout/liquidity/take/reaction lineage as it was knowable, then identify the selected entry TF/model and actual logical stop/target. Compare the stated personal choice against the model before evaluating outcome. A source missed/passed example should not become a claimed personal fill; a source loss must remain a loss observation, and an open trade must remain open.

Exact source-to-IC alignment, independently established setup/fill UTC windows, numerical initial stop/entry/target tolerances and broker execution/management are unresolved for all 17 episodes. Spoken approximate R and repositioned drawing-box R are not identical accounting facts. The inventory makes no PASS claim and adds no engine-generated expectations. GBPCAD's visible June chart can justify an explicitly declared exploratory view window, not an inferred exact fill time or a demonstrated Weekly-veto rule.
